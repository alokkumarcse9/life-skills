# Good Practices for Software Development

## 1. Which point(s) were new to you?

* If the requirements are not clear, we should take frequent feedback from the team during implementation.
* If there are any problems or delays, we should inform the team early instead of waiting until the problem is solved.
* When we are stuck, we should clearly explain the problem and mention the solutions we have already tried.
* We can use screenshots, diagrams, and code snippets to explain problems more easily.
* We should respect our team members' time and avoid sending unnecessary multiple messages.
* Joining meetings 5-10 minutes early and spending some time with teammates can help improve communication.
* We should avoid distractions during work and stay fully involved in our work.

## 2. Which area do you think you need to improve on? What are your ideas to make progress in that area?

I need to improve my communication skills and become more active while working with my team. Sometimes I spend too much time trying to solve a problem on my own instead of asking for help. I also need to improve how I explain my problems and give updates. To improve, I will ask questions when the requirements are unclear, share regular updates with my team, participate more actively in meetings, and use screenshots or diagrams when needed to explain my work clearly.
