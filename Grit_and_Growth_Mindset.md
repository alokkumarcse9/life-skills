# Grit and Growth Mindset

## 1. What is Grit? (Summary)

* Grit is the combination of passion and perseverance for achieving long-term goals.
* It is like running a marathon, not a sprint.
* Grit means staying focused and continuing to work even when things become difficult.

## 2. What is a Growth Mindset? (Summary)

* A growth mindset is a way of thinking where we believe that our abilities and intelligence can be developed through effort, practice, and learning.
* We use feedback as an opportunity to improve instead of taking it personally.
* We see learning new things as an opportunity, not a burden.
* Skills are built over time, and anyone can learn and grow with consistent effort.
* A growth mindset is about getting a little better every day through continuous learning and practice.

## 3. What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

* Internal locus of control means believing that your actions, choices, and efforts can influence your results instead of depending on luck or other people.
* The key point of the video is that taking responsibility for your actions and focusing on solving problems can help you stay motivated and achieve your goals.
