# Focus Management

## 1. What is Deep Work?

* Deep Work means focusing completely on a difficult task without distractions.
* It helps us learn faster, improve skills, and produce high-quality work.

## 2. According to author how to do deep work properly, in a few points?

* Work for 60–90 minutes without distractions.
* Avoid switching between different tasks.
* Use time blocking and follow a regular Deep Work routine.
* Plan your next day's work and use a shutdown ritual at the end of the day.

## 3. How can you implement the principles in your day-to-day life?

* Keep a fixed time for focused work every day.
* Turn off notifications and keep your phone away while working.
* Check emails and social media at fixed times.
* Focus on one task at a time.
* Plan your tasks before starting the day.

## 4. What are the dangers of social media, in brief?

* Social media distracts us from important work.
* It breaks our concentration and makes Deep Work difficult.
* It can cause procrastination and reduce productivity.
* Excessive use can affect our ability to focus for long periods.
* It can also contribute to anxiety, loneliness, and dissatisfaction.
