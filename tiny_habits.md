# Tiny Habits

## 1. Tiny Habits: - What was the most interesting story or idea for you?

For me, the most interesting idea was Tiny Habits and celebrating after completing a tiny habit. Instead of trying to make a big change at once, we should start with a very small habit, like doing 2 push-ups or solving one problem. Celebrating after completing it makes us feel successful and helps us repeat the habit. Slowly, it becomes a part of our daily life.

## 2. How can you use B = MAP to make making new habits easier? What are M, A and P.

- **M = Motivation:** The desire or reason to do something.

- **A = Ability:** Make the habit very small and easy to do.

- **P = Prompt:** A trigger that reminds you to do the habit. For example, "After I do this, I will do my new habit."

To make a new habit easier, start with a very small task and connect it to something you already do every day. This makes it easier to repeat and slowly turn it into a habit.

## 3. Why it is important to "Shine" or Celebrate after each successful completion of habit? (This is the most important concept in today's topic. Whatever you celebrate becomes a habit)

Celebrating after completing a habit gives our brain a positive feeling and makes us feel proud and successful. This makes us want to repeat the habit again, and slowly it becomes a regular part of our daily life.

## 4. (1% Better Every Day Video), what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was getting 1% better every day. Small improvements may not show big results immediately, but they add up over time. Instead of trying to become perfect in one day, it is better to improve a little every day. These small improvements can make a big difference over time.

## 5. What is the book's perspective about Identity?

The book says that our habits help create our identity. Every good habit is a small step toward becoming the person we want to be. For example, if we practice coding every day, we slowly start seeing ourselves as a programmer.

## 6. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit easier to do?

- Start with a very small habit.
- Make the habit simple and easy to start.
- Keep everything ready before starting.
- Use the 2-minute rule and focus on just starting.


## 7. Write about the book's perspective on how to make a habit harder to do?

To reduce a bad habit, make it more difficult and less attractive.

- Remove the cue or reminder.
- Make the habit less attractive.
- Add more steps before doing it.
- Make it less satisfying.
