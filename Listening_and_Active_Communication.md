# Communication Skills – Reflection Questions

## 1. What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

* I should give my full attention to the speaker.
* I should avoid distractions and focus on the conversation.
* I should not interrupt while the other person is speaking.
* I should let them finish before giving my response.
* I should use body language like eye contact and nodding to show that I am listening.
* I can take notes when the conversation is important.
* If I don't understand something, I should ask about it after the speaker finishes.

## 2. According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

* I should listen carefully and understand what the other person is saying.
* I should repeat or explain the main point in my own words to make sure I understood it correctly.
* If something is not clear, I should ask questions instead of making assumptions.
* The main goal is to make sure both people understand the same thing.

## 3. What are the obstacles in your listening process?

* I am generally a good listener, but sometimes I interrupt when I don't understand something.
* Sometimes I start thinking about my response while the other person is still speaking.
* I can also make assumptions instead of asking for clarification.
* Interrupting can break the speaker's flow, so I need to work on this.

## 4. What can you do to improve your listening?

* I will try to listen until the person finishes speaking.
* If I don't understand something, I will ask politely instead of interrupting.
* I can say, "I didn't understand that point. Could you please explain it again?"
* I will also avoid distractions and pay more attention during important conversations.

## 5. When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

* Sometimes I become passive when someone needs my help.
* I may stop my own work and priorities to help them solve their problem.
* I sometimes find it difficult to say no, especially when I feel that someone genuinely needs help.
* This does not happen with everyone, but it can happen with people close to me.

## 6. When do you switch into Aggressive communication style in your day-to-day life?

* I usually try to handle situations politely.
* But when someone repeatedly ignores what I am saying or does not try to understand my point, I can become frustrated.
* In such situations, my tone can become more aggressive.
* I need to learn to stay calm and explain my point without becoming too aggressive.

## 7. When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication style?

* When I am angry or unhappy with someone, I sometimes prefer to stay quiet instead of talking about the problem.
* I usually start talking normally again after my anger goes down.
* Sometimes staying silent can make the situation worse because the other person may not understand what is bothering me.
* I should try to communicate the problem directly and calmly instead.

## 8. How can you make your communication assertive?

* I usually express my feelings directly instead of speaking indirectly.
* However, sometimes the way I speak can hurt other people's feelings.
* I want to improve this by choosing my words more carefully.
* I will try to clearly explain what I feel without blaming or disrespecting others.
* I will also listen to the other person's point of view and try to find a solution that works for both sides.
