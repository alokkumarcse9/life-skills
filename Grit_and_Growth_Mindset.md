# Grit and Growth Mindset

## Question 1

### Paraphrase the Grit video in 1–2 lines.

The video explains that success is not only about intelligence or talent. Grit means staying focused on a long-term goal and continuing to work hard even when things become difficult or when we fail.

---

## Question 2

### Paraphrase the Introduction to Growth Mindset video in 1–2 lines.

The video explains that a growth mindset means believing that our skills and abilities can improve with effort, practice, and learning. Instead of giving up when something is difficult, we should see it as a chance to learn and improve.

---

## Question 3

### What is the Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions, choices, and effort can affect the results we get.

The main point is that instead of blaming luck, other people, or situations, we should focus on what we can control and what we can do to improve the situation. The video also explains that taking responsibility can help us stay motivated.

---

## Question 4

### What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a growth mindset?

* Believe that you can improve.
* Believe that you can find a way to solve problems.
* Accept challenges.
* Do not be afraid of failure.
* Learn from your mistakes.
* Accept that struggling is part of learning.
* Keep trying when things become difficult.
* Focus on effort and improvement.
* Do not give up because of temporary problems.
* Keep learning and growing.

---

## Question 5

### What are your ideas to take action and build Growth Mindset?

I can build a growth mindset by treating my mistakes as learning opportunities instead of getting discouraged by them. As a Software Developer, I will practice regularly, take feedback from others, and spend more time on the topics that I find difficult.

I will try to improve step by step instead of expecting to become perfect quickly. I will also remind myself that "I cannot do it yet" is better than saying "I cannot do it."

Most importantly, I will stay consistent and keep trying even when something is challenging.
