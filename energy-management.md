# Energy Management

## 1. What are the activities you do that help you relax (Calm Quadrant)?

* Sleeping when I have enough time.
* Taking a short break and taking slow, deep breaths.

## 2. When do you find yourself getting into the Stress Quadrant?

* When someone wakes me up while I am sleeping.
* When I get new information that is not in my favor, I feel stressed at that moment. I usually manage it within a few days if I can do something about it. If I cannot change the situation, I accept it and move on.

## 3. How do you know if you are in the Excitement Quadrant?

* I become more talkative when I am excited.
* I usually try to control my excitement and stay calm instead of showing it too much.

# Sleep is Your Superpower

## 4. Paraphrase the Sleep is your Superpower video in your own words in brief. Only the points, no explanation.

* Sleep helps our brain remember what we learn.
* Good sleep helps us think more clearly and stay focused.
* Deep sleep helps our brain store memories for a long time.
* Getting enough sleep is important for both our brain and body.
* Good sleep can help us stay healthier and live longer.

## 5. What are some ideas that you can implement to sleep better?

* I already sleep well, but I want to fall asleep faster.
* I can exercise regularly to help me fall asleep more easily.
* I can maintain a regular sleep and wake-up time.
* I can keep my room cool and comfortable before going to sleep.
* I can avoid using my phone or doing other stimulating activities when I am trying to sleep.

# Brain-Changing Benefits of Exercise

## 6. Paraphrase the video - Brain Changing Benefits of Exercise. Minimum 5 points, only the points.

* Regular exercise can create new brain cells in the hippocampus.
* Exercise can improve long-term memory.
* A single workout can improve mood and energy.
* Exercise can increase neurotransmitters like dopamine, serotonin, and noradrenaline.
* Exercise can improve focus and attention.
* Regular exercise can help protect the brain from cognitive decline.

## 7. What are some steps you can take to exercise more?

* I need to stop worrying too much about getting a job.
* I just need to start exercising instead of thinking about it too much.
* I can start with small workouts and slowly make them a regular habit.
* I am good at maintaining consistency once I start, so my main goal is to take the first step.
* I can include simple activities like walking, taking the stairs, or doing basic exercises in my daily routine.
