# Learning Process

## 1. What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a way of learning where you explain a concept in simple words, as if you are teaching it to someone who does not know anything about it.

## 2. In this video, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was that we should test ourselves again and again instead of only reading the same material. When we try to remember something without looking at our notes, we understand and remember it better.

## 3. What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

1. Active mode is when we focus on learning, understanding, or solving a problem.
2. Diffused mode is when we take a break and relax, allowing our brain to connect ideas in the background.

## 4. According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Break the skill or topic into smaller parts.
2. Learn enough to start practising and correct your own mistakes.
3. Remove distractions and other barriers to practice.
4. Practise for at least 20 hours.
