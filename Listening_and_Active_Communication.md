# Communication Skills – Reflection Questions


## Question 1
### What are the steps/strategies to do Active Listening?

- Focus on the speaker and topic.
- Avoid distractions.
- Do not interrupt.
- Let the speaker finish.
- Use body language to show attention.
- Take notes when needed.
- Paraphrase to confirm understanding.
- Ask questions when something is unclear.

---

## Question 2
### According to Fisher's model, what are the key points of Reflective Listening?

- Listen carefully and understand the message.
- Take notes during important discussions.
- Repeat the main idea in your own words.
- Verify that you understood correctly.
- Clarify doubts and mistakes.
- Make sure everyone is on the same page.
- Use reflective listening in technical discussions.

---

## Question 3
### What are the obstacles in your listening process?

- Distractions.
- Overthinking.
- Interrupting others.
- Making assumptions.
- Lack of concentration.
- Strong emotions.
- Preparing my response while others are speaking.
- Not asking for clarification.

---

## Question 4
### What can you do to improve your listening?

- Give full attention to the speaker.
- Avoid distractions.
- Do not interrupt.
- Take notes when needed.
- Ask questions when unclear.
- Paraphrase important points.
- Use positive body language.
- Avoid assumptions.

---

## Question 5
### When do you switch to Passive communication style in your day-to-day life?

- When I want to avoid conflict.
- When I am afraid of hurting someone.
- When I find it difficult to say no.
- When I lack confidence.
- When I think others' needs are more important.
- When I stay quiet about my feelings.

---

## Question 6
### When do you switch into Aggressive communication styles in your day-to-day life?

- When I am very angry or frustrated.
- When someone repeatedly ignores me.
- When I feel someone is taking advantage of me.
- When I lose patience.
- When I strongly disagree with someone.
- When I raise my voice during an argument.

---

## Question 7
### When do you switch into Passive-Aggressive communication styles?

- When I am angry but don't speak directly.
- When I use sarcasm or taunts.
- When I give someone the silent treatment.
- When I gossip instead of addressing the problem.
- When I say "It's fine" even when I am upset.
- When I deliberately avoid something because I am unhappy.

---

## Question 8
### How can you make your communication assertive?

- Recognize and name my feelings.
- Understand what I actually need.
- Express my needs clearly and honestly.
- Avoid blaming others.
- Practice in low-stakes situations.
- Keep my tone and body language calm.
- Speak up early about problems.
- Learn to say "No" respectfully.
- Respect both my needs and others' needs.
- Practice assertive communication regularly.
